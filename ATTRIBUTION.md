# Outbound link attribution

Every external link from this site uses:

- `utm_source=mystrowin.com`
- `utm_medium=referral`
- `utm_campaign=mystrowin_portfolio`
- `utm_content=<page/link identifier>`

External links also explicitly preserve the normal cross-origin referrer policy:

`referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"`

This lets destination analytics identify Mystrowin as the referring site while
`utm_content` distinguishes where the click originated.
