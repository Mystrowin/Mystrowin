About Mystrowin

Mystrowin is the developer identity connecting software projects, systems experiments, technical research, and public releases.

Identity One name across the work.

Arqivexa is the main active project, while Mystrowin remains the umbrella identity for the broader technical portfolio.

Why Mystrowin exists

Rather than presenting every experiment as an unrelated project, Mystrowin provides a consistent developer identity for software, systems work, and technical exploration.

The goal is simple: build technically ambitious software, document it clearly, and keep a coherent public record of the work as it develops.
